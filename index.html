About Me

My journey in education

"I believe teaching is not about perfection—it's about connection, adaptability, and continuous learning."

I am a Physics Educator who values practical, real-world teaching over rigid theory. My classroom approach is flexible, student-centered, and focused on making science meaningful in everyday life.
